Depending on your partnership type, you have access to different sets of loan products:

### 1. API Integration Products

API partners gain access to high-demand, fully digital loan products for instant online journey integrations.

  </Card>

  <Card title="Pocket Loan" icon="zap">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Gold Loan" icon="coins">

### 2. Traditional DSA Products

DSA partners enjoy the **complete product portfolio** to refer any loan type and cater to a wider client base.

- **Unsecured Credit:** Personal Loans, Unsecured Business Loans, MSME Loans
- **Secured Credit:** Secured Business Loans, Home Loans, Loan Against Property (LAP), Loan Against Securities (LAS)
- **Asset/Vehicle Finance:** New Car Loans, Used Car Loans, Machinery Financing

## Who Should Choose What?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="API Partnership: For Tech Platforms & FinTech" icon="code">
    Choose this if you have a software application, digital CRM, customer portal, or website and want to build a seamless embedded lending experience.

    **Key workflow:**

    1. Customer applies on your portal.
    2. Your backend triggers the API request.
    3. EasyCred's smart routing engine forwards to 40\+ lenders.
    4. Loan offers are returned instantly via API.
    5. Fast, paperless disbursal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="DSA / Channel Partner: For Financial Consultants" icon="users">
    Choose this if you prefer a ready-made, zero-code web portal to manually upload client details, submit documents, review lender offers, and track payouts.

    **Key workflow:**

    1. Submit client lead manually in the Partner Portal.
    2. A dedicated EASYCRED Relationship Manager (RM) is assigned.
    3. The RM assists with documentation and coordinate with banks.
    4. The loan is disbursed.
    5. You earn high commissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
