## Overview

When an application reaches `OFFERS_RECEIVED` status, eligible credit offers are available for review. Partners can inspect details with the applicant and confirm their selection to proceed with verification and disbursal.

---

## Offer Details

Each offer card provides key loan terms:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Approved Principal" icon="indian-rupee">
    Approved credit limit or loan amount sanctioned for the applicant.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Monthly EMI & Tenure" icon="calculator">
    Projected monthly installment amount and repayment tenure.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Interest Rate & APR" icon="percent">
    Annual interest rate and inclusive Annual Percentage Rate (APR).
  </Card>

  <Card title="Net Disbursal Amount" icon="wallet">
    Estimated proceeds disbursed to bank account after deducting applicable processing fees.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Selecting an Offer

1. **Review:** Present the available credit options to the customer.
2. **Confirm:** Click **Select This Offer** on the preferred card. The application moves to `OFFER_SELECTED`.
3. **Verification Steps:** The applicant is guided to complete KYC verification, e-NACH mandate registration, and digital agreement signing.

<Note>
  Offer selection is final and cannot be modified once confirmed.
</Note>

---

## Secondary & Referral Offers

If primary credit parameters require alternative solutions, secondary referral offers (such as Apollo Finvest) may be presented to the applicant:
- In the portal and hosted journeys, referral offers appear with direct actions to evaluate specialized credit options.
- In headless API journeys, these options are delivered in `data.referralOffers[]` for display in your custom application.
