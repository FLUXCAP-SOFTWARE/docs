## Account Onboarding

EASYCRED partner registration is **invitation-only** to ensure compliance with RBI digital lending guidelines.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive Your Invitation">
    An EASYCRED administrator sends a secure tokenized email invite to your registered email address.

    ```text
    https://partner.easycred.co.in/register?token=wh_xxxx
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Registration Form">
    Fill in the following details on the registration form:

    - **Company details** — business name, type, and registration number
    - **Ownership type** — Individual, Sole Proprietor, or Private Limited
    - **Registered physical address**
    - **Banking credentials** — account details for commission payouts
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admin Review & Approval">
    Once submitted, your application moves to `PENDING` status. The EASYCRED admin team performs verification and approves your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your DSA Code">
    Upon approval, you are automatically issued a unique **DSA Code** (e.g., `EC-DSA-0010`) that identifies your partner account across the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The invitation link is single-use and time-limited. Contact your EASYCRED onboarding manager if your link has expired.
</Warning>

## Authentication (OTP-based)

To prevent password leaks and phishing, authentication is **entirely passwordless** — using a one-time code sent to your registered email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Login Screen">
    Go to [partners.easycred.co.in](https://partners.easycred.co.in) and enter your registered email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Your OTP">
    Click **Send OTP**. A 6-digit code is dispatched to your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and Sign In">
    Enter the 6-digit OTP in the verification field and click **Verify & Sign In** to start your session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  OTPs expire after a short window. If your code has expired, return to the login screen and request a fresh OTP.
</Info>

## Account Status Reference

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PENDING` | Registration submitted, awaiting admin verification |
| `APPROVED` | Account verified — DSA Code issued, portal access granted |
| `SUSPENDED` | Account temporarily restricted — contact support |
| `REVOKED` | Account permanently deactivated |
