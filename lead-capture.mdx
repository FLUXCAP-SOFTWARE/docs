## Overview

Partners can manually submit new loan leads directly from the portal. The platform automatically routes each lead to the appropriate lender channel based on the loan type selected.

---

## Lead Submission Form

### Personal Details

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| First & Last Name | Customer's full legal name |
| Date of Birth | Used for eligibility and KYC |
| Mobile Number | 10-digit number — used for OTP and lender communications |
| Email Address | Used for journey notifications |
| PAN | Auto-formatted to uppercase (e.g., `ABCDE1234F`) |

### Employment Information

| Field | Options / Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Employment Type | **Salaried** or **Self-Employed** |
| Monthly Net Income | After-tax take-home salary |
| Company Name | Employer name (for salaried applicants) |

### Loan Criteria

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Requested Loan Amount | Desired disbursement amount in ₹ |
| Loan Type | Personal, Business, Gold, LAP, Home, Car, Education, Working Capital |

## Automatic Lender Routing

The platform automatically selects the lender channel based on loan type — you don't need to configure this:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Personal, Business & Gold Loans" icon="building-2">
    Routes to **EASYCRED adapters**. The journey is initiated programmatically and the lead moves through the standard headless flow (`INITIATED` → `OFFERS_RECEIVED` → …).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Home, Car & Education Loans" icon="house">
    Assigned to **offline operations**. An EASYCRED operations team member contacts the customer to complete the application.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Working Capital Loans" icon="briefcase">
    Assigned to **offline operations** and handled by the EASYCRED B2B lending desk.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## After Submission

Once submitted, the lead appears in your **Leads** table with the status `NEW`. The platform then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assigns Routing Rules">
    The system evaluates the loan type and customer profile to select the appropriate lender channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transmits to Lender">
    The lead is sent to the lender's credit check system. Status moves to `INITIATED`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Returns Offers">
    For online journeys, lender credit offers are returned (`OFFERS_RECEIVED`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Ensure the customer's PAN and mobile number are accurate before submitting — incorrect details are the most common cause of lender rejections.
</Tip>
