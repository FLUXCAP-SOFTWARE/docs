## Overview

Partners can submit new customer loan applications directly from the EASYCRED Partner Portal. Depending on the product chosen, the application is processed either through the automated digital journey or assigned to a dedicated lending desk.

---

## Lead Information Required

### Applicant Details
- **Full Name:** Customer's legal name as per official records
- **Mobile Number:** 10-digit number for verification and status updates
- **Email Address:** Contact email for application updates
- **PAN:** 10-character PAN (`ABCDE1234F`)
- **Date of Birth:** `YYYY-MM-DD`

### Financial Profile
- **Employment Type:** Salaried or Self-Employed
- **Monthly Net Income / Annual Turnover:** Take-home salary or business turnover
- **Company Name:** Employer name (salaried) or trade name (self-employed)

### Loan Requirements
- **Requested Amount:** Desired funding amount
- **Loan Category:** Personal, Pocket, Business, Gold, LAP, Home, Car, Education, or Working Capital

## Application Processing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Digital Loans (Personal, Pocket, Business, Gold)" icon="zap">
    Processed through the EASYCRED digital origination journey. Eligible applications receive instant offers and move through online verification.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Desk-Managed Loans (Home, Car, Education, LAP, Working Capital)" icon="building">
    Assigned directly to dedicated EASYCRED credit desks for physical documentation, legal appraisal, and offline fulfillment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Tracking After Submission

Submitted applications immediately appear in your **Leads & Applications** table with real-time status updates from initial submission to final disbursal.
