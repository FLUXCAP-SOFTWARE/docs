## Overview

All commission slabs, real-time earnings, approval stages, and bank transfer records are tracked and managed directly within the **EASYCRED Partner Portal**.

When a loan application is successfully sanctioned and disbursed, the corresponding commission entry appears in your partner dashboard with full lifecycle tracking:

- **Pending:** Loan disbursed; awaiting operational ledger verification.
- **Approved:** Commission verified and queued for the next bank transfer run.
- **Paid:** Payout completed to your registered bank account with a bank UTR number.
