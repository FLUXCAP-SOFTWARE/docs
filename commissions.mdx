## Overview

The **Commissions** screen tracks the complete payout lifecycle for every sanctioned loan application linked to your partner account.

---

## Commission Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Commission Generated">
    When a lead reaches `SANCTIONED` status (loan disbursed), an earnings entry is automatically created in your commission ledger with `PENDING` status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Operational Review">
    The EASYCRED operations team reviews and verifies the commission entry against the lender disbursal confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approved for Payment">
    Verified commissions are marked `APPROVED` and queued for the next bank run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Transferred">
    Funds are transferred to your registered bank account. The entry moves to `PAID` with a UTR reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Commission Structures

Commission rates are **partner-specific** and are configured by the EASYCRED admin team at the time of onboarding. Three structure types are supported:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Fixed — Flat amount per loan" icon="hash">
    A fixed rupee amount is paid per successfully disbursed loan, regardless of the loan amount.

    **Example:** ₹500 per sanctioned loan

    Best for: Simple, predictable payout tracking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Percentage — % of disbursed principal" icon="percent">
    A percentage of the net disbursed loan amount is paid as commission.

    **Example:** 1.5% of the disbursed principal

    | Disbursed Amount | Commission @ 1.5% |
    | --- | --- |
    | ₹1,00,000 | ₹1,500 |
    | ₹5,00,000 | ₹7,500 |
    | ₹10,00,000 | ₹15,000 |

    Best for: Partners incentivized to maximize loan amounts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slabs — Tiered rates by loan size" icon="layers">
    Different rates apply to different disbursement bands, giving higher rates for larger loans.

    **Example slab structure:**

    | Disbursed Amount | Commission Rate |
    | --- | --- |
    | Up to ₹5,00,000 | 1.0% |
    | ₹5,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 1.5% |
    | Above ₹10,00,000 | 2.0% |

    Best for: Partners with diverse loan size portfolios.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Your commission structure is defined at onboarding and applied automatically — you don't need to manually calculate payouts. Contact your EASYCRED relationship manager to discuss structure changes.
</Note>

## Tracking Payouts

Each commission entry in the **Paid** tab includes:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Application ID** | The loan application this commission relates to |
| **Disbursed Amount** | The loan principal amount used to calculate the commission |
| **Commission Amount** | The net commission credited to your account |
| **Payment Date** | Date the bank transfer was initiated |
| **UTR Reference** | Unique Transaction Reference for the bank transfer |

<Tip>
  Use the UTR Reference to reconcile commission payments in your own accounting records.
</Tip>
