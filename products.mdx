## Overview

Your API key has access to a configured set of loan products via `productAccess`. When initiating a loan journey, you must pass one of the supported product codes.

## Product-Specific Payload Requirements

### 1. Personal Loan (`ONLINE_PERSONAL`) & Pocket Loan (`ONLINE_SHORT_TERM`)
Standard applicant parameters inside the `customer` object:
- `pan`, `dob`, `gender`, `personalEmail`, `mobile`, `pinCode`, `termsAccepted`
- `employmentType`: `"salaried"` or `"selfEmployed"`
- `monthlyIncome` (or `annualIncome`)

### 2. Business Loan (`ONLINE_BUSINESS`)
In addition to standard personal fields, business loan journeys require:
- `customer.companyName`: Registered enterprise or trade name
- `customer.employmentType`: Must be `"selfEmployed"`
- `customer.annualIncome`: Annual business turnover / gross income

### 3. Gold Loan (`ONLINE_GOLD`)
Requires a dedicated `goldLoanDetails` object in the initiate request:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `jewellaryList` | `string[]` | Yes | List of gold articles, e.g. `["Gold Bangle 22K", "Gold Chain 22K"]` |
| `grams` | `string` | Yes | Total gross gold weight in grams, e.g. `"35.5"` |
